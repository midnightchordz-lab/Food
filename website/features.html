More than a recipe box

A kitchen that adapts to you.

From the first "I don't know what to eat" to a shared shopping list for the whole family — here's everything MoodFood's AI chef does.

AI chef chat

Tell it how you feel, what you're craving, or what's stressing you out — it holds a real conversation and matches recipes to the mood, not a filter menu.

Diabetes-friendly planning

Net carbs and glycemic index are built into every suggestion, so a meal plan can support the way you actually need to eat.

Import any recipe

Paste a link, snap a photo of a cookbook page, drop in a YouTube video, or paste raw text — MoodFood turns it into a recipe it understands.

Fridge scanner

Photograph what's actually in your fridge and get recipes built around it — no more staring at vegetables with no plan.

Voice-guided cooking

Hands-free, step-by-step instructions while you actually cook — no wiping flour off your phone screen.

Weekly meal planner

Plan the week around how you'll feel, not just what's easy — breakfast through dessert, filtered by diet and cuisine.

Smart shopping lists

Built automatically from your plan, so the only decision left at the store is which aisle to start in.

Family sharing

Invite your household in — plan, shop and cook together, with everyone's moods and diets accounted for.
