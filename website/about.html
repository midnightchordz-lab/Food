Our mission

A culinary experience that feels like a hug, not a spreadsheet.

MoodFood exists to create a warm, compassionate, mood-responsive way to eat — one that puts how you're feeling before what's trending.

What we believe

Four ideas guide everything we build.

Nutrition, emotion, and editorial food writing

MoodFood sits at the intersection of nutritional science, emotional comfort, and the kind of food writing you'd actually want to read.

Emotion first

Every interaction acknowledges how you're feeling before it offers a solution. The mood comes first, the recipe comes second.

Content is king

Recipes are stories, not just ingredient lists — the way food writing should have always worked.

Room to breathe

Generous negative space, calm pacing, no clutter — designed to lower cognitive load, especially on a stressed-out day.

Light, warm, and a little optimistic.

MoodFood's whole look — Warm Bone backgrounds, Sage Green calm, a Terracotta spark of warmth — is built to mimic natural light and fresh ingredients. Even the evening "Dim" mode stays a soft warm grey instead of pitch black, because rest shouldn't feel harsh either.
